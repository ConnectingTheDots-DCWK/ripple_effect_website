This is the first item in a feed the app reads itself. From Ripple Effect
1.5.1, the home screen has a **News** button, and a number on it when
something here is new since you last looked.

## What it is for

Some news isn't in the editor's release notes, because it isn't about the
editor. The engine Ripple Effect plays campaigns with is also published for
other people's software, and the hosts built on it ship on their own schedule:

- [`@ripplefx/player` and `@ripplefx/soundscape`](https://github.com/ConnectingTheDots-DCWK/ripple_effect_js_ts_integration)
  on npm, which play a campaign and its soundscapes in a web page.
- [The C and C++ binding](https://github.com/ConnectingTheDots-DCWK/ripple_effect_c_cpp_integration),
  for native games and engines.

A new binding, an integration, or a documentation page that has moved has
nowhere to go in a changelog, so it goes here. The same goes for anything you
need to do: a step before an update, or a change in how something is
installed.

You don't need an update to get the news, an account, or a visit to this
website. A news item is published here and appears on your home screen the
next time the app starts.

## What it costs you

Nothing about you. Once per launch the app asks this site for the feed. The
request carries no identifier, no version number and no cookie. Any picture
with an item comes from this site too, and from nowhere else. The app
remembers which items you've read on your machine, and never sends it.

If you'd rather not have it, switch off **News** under **Settings ▸ Updates**.
That stops the request at once. The [Privacy
Notice](https://legal.ripplefx.app/privacy/), §4.7, is the formal version of
this paragraph.

If you accepted version 1.0 of the licence, the app tells you that version 2.0
exists. It asks you to accept 2.0 no sooner than thirty days later. Until you
do, 1.0 is what governs you, and the app doesn't fetch the news.

The same items are also at [ripplefx.app/news](/news), with an RSS feed for any
reader that wants it.
