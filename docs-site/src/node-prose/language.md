Words and facts in the reader's language. Both cards answer for the language the reader is reading in, or the one their `locale` picker or a wire names, and take the flow to do it: a value is in the language it was read in. Where nothing is written in the reader's language, a Localized string answers in the language the story is written in. See [How to add, switch off or change a project's languages](/how-to/manage-languages/).
