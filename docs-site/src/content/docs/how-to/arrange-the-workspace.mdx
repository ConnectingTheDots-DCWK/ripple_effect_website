## Put two documents side by side

- To put a board beside the passage it links to, drag its tab out of the strip and drop it beside the other document.
- To tab a document into a group instead, drop it on that group's strip.
- To resize, drag a divider.

Your layout is remembered per project in `.ripple/workspace.json`.

## Give the documents the whole width

<Shot name="files" />

Click the activity bar icon of the section already showing — **Files**, **Changes** or **Search** — to fold the side bar away. Click the icon of any section to pick what the side bar shows again.

## Close and reopen Messages

- Close Messages with its cross.
- Bring it back, under the documents, from the Messages button at the bottom of the activity bar, which carries the count, from the **View** menu, or with *Toggle messages* in the command palette (`Ctrl+Shift+P`).

To change how Messages reads:

- To see everything broken first, switch the grouping from what a message is about to how loud it is.
- To fold a group, click the count on its heading.
- To hide a kind of message, turn off its chip: problems, cautions or records. The grouping and the chips are remembered.
- To silence a deliberate choice the check cannot judge, tick *I meant that* on its row. It stays said.
- To read about a message from the engine, click its code to open its page in [Diagnostics](/reference/diagnostics/). To find the cards a message names, click the message.

## Give the window to one document

Press `F11` (`Ctrl+Cmd+F` on a Mac), or choose *View ▸ Zen Mode*. The app bar, side bar, Messages and tabs go. The same keys bring them back.

- A banner at the top names the document on the way in and whenever it changes. When the pointer reaches the top edge it carries an *Exit Zen Mode* button.
- `Ctrl+Tab` and `Ctrl+Shift+Tab` move between documents — every open one, whichever group it is in. Outside Zen Mode the same keys move along the tabs of the group you are in.

## Bring the tips back

Each tip appears once, the first time you open its kind of thing. To see one again, run *Show the tip for…* from the command palette. To re-arm all of them, choose *Help ▸ Troubleshooting ▸ Show every tip again*.
