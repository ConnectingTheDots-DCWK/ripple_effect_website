## Play a board

<Steps>

1. Open the board and press **Play** at its bottom right. The campaign window opens and runs the story from that board's Start, following Jumps from board to board until a path reaches an End.

   <Shot name="play" />

2. Read the passage. It is rendered as Markdown with its Python already run, and the choices leading out of it are the tiles beneath. Press a tile to follow it.

3. Press **Start over** when you want to run again. It is available from the first passage to an ending, and an ending also offers *Play again*.

</Steps>

Play re-indexes the project before it compiles, so the playthrough is current without exporting first. [Why the preview is the shipped story](/explanation/the-preview-is-the-shipped-story/) explains why.

## Read in another language

Pick the language from the campaign window's header. You can change it mid-passage. A passage with nothing in the chosen language says so instead of falling back to another.

## Answer an Input node's form

When the story reaches an **Input** node, fill in the form and press Enter in a field to continue. The fields are listed in [The campaign window and saved games](/reference/campaign-window/).

## Let a story read the time or the location

A story that reads the date and time or where you are asks before it begins, unless you have already answered.

<Steps>

1. To answer ahead of time, open Settings, choose **Permissions**, and pick *Ask*, *Allow* or *Deny* for *Location* and for *Date and time*. Type a stand-in under either to have a story told that instead when it cannot have the real one, such as `2026-10-19 18:30` or `Rome, Italy`.

2. Press **Play**. If a kind the story reads is still on *Ask*, the window opens on *Before this story begins*: press *Allow* or *Don't allow* on each row. Your answers are kept under Settings › Permissions.

3. If you allowed the location, the window shows *Finding where you are…* for up to a few seconds. Press *Begin without it* to start at once.

4. If a strip appears across the top of the window, the story was given your stand-in, was refused something it read, or was allowed something this computer had nothing of. Look its code up in the [diagnostics reference](/reference/diagnostics/#irt05), and press the cross (*Dismiss*) at its end to close it.

</Steps>

What a script is handed in each case is in [The world outside the story](/reference/python/the-world/).

## Deal with warnings

Compile warnings, such as a roll that cannot vary or a macro missing one of its ends, appear in **Messages** when you press Play, not only when you export. The engine's board checks appear there too: Play checks every board, and a finding names the cards it concerns.

<Steps>

1. Open **Messages** and read the finding. Look the code up in the [diagnostics reference](/reference/diagnostics/).

2. Fix the board, or, for `W.GE.06`, choose **I meant that** on the finding to acknowledge it for that card.

</Steps>

If the project has turned **Python** off (project settings, *Capabilities*), every Script node and every passage with Python in it is marked as an error and listed in Messages. The campaign will not play until none remain.

## Raise the loop ceiling

A loop nothing breaks out of stops the campaign and offers you the node that ran away. By default one loop node may go round 512 times in a board.

<Steps>

1. Open Settings and find **Playing**.

2. Choose 128, 512, 2,048, 8,192, or None for no ceiling.

</Steps>

A For node's own *Max iterations* still applies first when it is lower.

## Play from a node

<Steps>

1. Right-click any node on a board.

2. Choose **Play from here**. The campaign begins at that node instead of the Start.

3. Set up whatever the story should already have made, using the console in the campaign window. It may do everything a Script node may.

</Steps>

<Shot name="playFromHere" />

The run starts with nothing made yet, and the window says so. Only a Start begins a story on its own; Play from here is how you begin anywhere else.

## Inspect and change a playthrough

Open a panel from the narrow strip down the window's right edge: Entities, Variables, Console or Trace. What each shows is in [The campaign window and saved games](/reference/campaign-window/).

- To change a value by hand, open **Variables** and edit it.
- To read the story's own steps, open **Trace**. Use its toggle to show everything underneath.
- To keep the plain lines, press **Copy** in **Trace**.

<Shot name="tracePanel" />

### Find the node behind a trace line

<Steps>

1. Tap a line in **Trace**. The story pauses, the window closes, and the board opens with that node selected and centred.

2. Press Resume to bring the story back.

</Steps>

A line about something inside a macro takes you to the card that called it, since that is what is on the board.

## Change the window

A button in the header takes the window to the app's edges and back; Escape also brings it back. The **mute** button beside it is remembered between sessions.
