---

Passages and Markdown notes in English are checked as you type, by [Harper](https://writewithharper.com/). A passage is checked in its own language, and a note in the project's default language, so a note is checked only when that language is an English one. Text in another language is not checked, and neither is the Python in a passage. The checking happens on your machine: nothing you write is sent anywhere to be checked.

The colour of an underline says what it found: red for spelling, amber for grammar, blue for style.

## Take a suggestion

<Steps>

1. Right-click the underlined words, or put the caret in them and press **Ctrl+.** (**⌘.** on a Mac).
2. Pick a suggestion. It replaces the underlined words.

</Steps>

The suggestions sit over the app's right-click menu for text, the one the Markdown, Python and reference fields share, with **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste** and **Select all** and the shortcut beside each. The line at the top of the menu is the checker's explanation, in English as the checker wrote it. **Ignore** stops underlining that one until the project is closed.

## Teach the project a word

On a spelling underline, choose **Add to dictionary** in the same menu. The word stops being underlined everywhere in the project.

Names the project already has are words without being added: its files' names, its console variables, its host callbacks and its state machines' states.

## See or change the dictionary

<Steps>

1. Open Project settings from the bottom of the activity bar and pick *Writing*.
2. Under *Dictionary*, type a word in *Add a word* and press Enter or choose **Add**. One word at a time: a phrase with a space is refused.
3. Use *Find a word* to search the list, and the bin beside a word to take it out.
4. Save. Cancel puts every word back.

</Steps>

The dictionary is saved with the project, so everyone who opens it shares it. A dictionary saved by a newer version of the app is not shown or changed: update the app to edit it.

## Switch checking off

<Steps>

1. Press the gear in the app bar. The Settings dialog opens.
2. On the *General* page, set *Writing* to *Off*.

</Steps>

The command palette's **Toggle spelling and grammar** does the same. The words in a project's dictionary are kept.
