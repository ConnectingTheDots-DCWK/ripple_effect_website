*Language*, *Contrast* and *Colour vision* are settings of the app, not of a project, so they follow you from project to project. Each is a choice among a few named options and takes effect when you press it; there is no Apply.

:::note
Contrast and colour vision are also on the first page of a first launch, beside a small preview that changes as you choose.
:::

## Open the settings

<Steps>

1. Press the gear in the app bar. The Settings dialog opens.
2. On the *General* page, find the row you want: *Language*, *Contrast* or *Colour vision*. [Settings, accessibility and updates controls](/reference/settings/) shows where each row sits.

</Steps>

## Change the language

<Steps>

1. In *Language*, press the language you want. Each language is listed under its own name, so you can find yours even when the app is showing one you cannot read.
2. To follow the operating system instead, press *System*, which the app shows followed by the language it currently resolves to, in brackets.

</Steps>

Every menu, dialog, message and tip changes. A project's own text and languages are untouched; to change those, see [How to add, switch off or change a project's languages](/how-to/manage-languages/).

## Raise the contrast

In *Contrast*, press *More*. Press *Standard* to go back.

Text becomes firmer and controls gain edges you can find; the colours and layout stay the same. [Settings](/reference/settings/#contrast) has the full list.

Contrast is separate from the theme, so you can have dark and firmer at once.

## Recolour the app for a kind of colour blindness

In *Colour vision*, press the mode that matches yours:

- *Protan*: protanopia and protanomaly.
- *Deutan*: deuteranopia and deuteranomaly.
- *Tritan*: tritanopia and tritanomaly.

Press *Off* to return to the house colours.

The app's category colours change to a palette you can tell apart; [Settings](/reference/settings/#colour-vision) lists what each mode recolours. A few places still tell categories apart by hue alone; see [What the accessibility settings do and do not claim](/explanation/accessibility-limits/).

## Change the text size

Text size is set in your operating system, not in the app. For why the cards on a board do not follow it, see [Text size: the chrome follows, the cards do not](/explanation/accessibility-limits/#text-size-the-chrome-follows-the-cards-do-not).
