A story with a few hundred passages across a dozen boards cannot be read by
looking at it. Three views answer the questions that come up instead: *how do
the chapters connect*, *how can a reader get from here to there*, and *how
could a reader have arrived here at all*.

## Script navigation

<Shot name="overview" />

**Script navigation** — the first button in the left panel's footer — draws how
a project's boards hand the story to each other: one card per board, one
output per way out of it, every arrow labelled with the node it leaves by —
and, for a Jump into the middle of a chapter, where it lands: `Down → The
cellar`, so it no longer draws exactly like a jump to the chapter's beginning
— and markers where a thread begins and ends. Nothing on it can be moved or
rewired; the arrangement is worked out for you, and the same project lays out
identically every time you open it.

Where a board begins is the same question the runner asks — *which node is the
Start?* — so what the map draws is what the story does.

## Tracing a thread

<Shot name="trace" />

On a board, pick two passages and every route between them lights up in
colour while the rest of the board goes under a scrim, a hue per linear run,
so where the routes part is the thing you see. On the boards overview the
same gesture traces between two *chapters*.

**What leads here** asks the one-ended question on a single passage: every
route that arrives at it, from wherever the story begins.

Confirming a trace finishes it: the board keeps its routes with only the cross
beside them, the next click is an ordinary click, and two boards can each show
a trace at once. Editing the board drops it.

## The merged view

{/* TODO(shots): <Shot name="mergedTrace" /> */}

When a route arrives through a Jump the board cannot draw it, so the answer
opens a **merged view**: every board on the route on one canvas, with the
bookkeeping between two passages traversed rather than drawn. It is read-only,
like script navigation; the boards themselves are where things change.

A trace on a board and a merged view across boards do not mix: opening a
merged view takes every board's trace away, and while one is open a board's
two trace buttons wait, and say why, until it is closed.

## What an agent gets

The same three questions have MCP tools — `trace_between`, `trace_into` and
`board_links` — so an assistant can answer *is this ending reachable* without
sitting through the story. See [Working with an AI
assistant](/overviews/working-with-an-ai-assistant/).
