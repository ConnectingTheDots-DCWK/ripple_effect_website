**Ripple Effect — Sub-processors, version 1.0 (English)**
Effective 15 October 2026.

The third parties that process personal data on our behalf, what each one is
for, and where it does it.

This list exists separately from the [Privacy Notice](/privacy/) on purpose:
changing a supplier should not require reissuing a notice, and a notice that
has to be reissued to correct a supplier's name is a notice that stays wrong
for a while. The Privacy Notice points here, and here is where the list is
kept current.

---

## From the release that introduces the hosted service

These process nothing today. They are listed in advance so that the list is
complete before the processing starts rather than after.

| Provider | What it will do for us | Processing location | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase Inc.** | Authentication, the account database, and the record of which terms you accepted | *To be confirmed before publication* | Processor |
| **Amazon Web Services, Inc.** | Object storage for project media synchronised to the service | *To be confirmed before publication* | Processor |
| **Resend** (Plus Five Five, Inc.) | Delivers account e-mail: address confirmations, sign-in links, password resets and the notices we owe accountholders | United States | Processor |
| **Polar Software Inc.** | Merchant of Record: sells the subscription, collects payment, handles tax, issues receipts | United States | **Independent controller** for payment and billing data |
| *Convergence host* | The real-time collaboration server | *Not yet selected* | Processor |
| *AI providers* | Features that send content to a third party on an action you take | *Not yet selected* | Processor |

**Polar is not our processor.** It is the seller of record and decides, as a
controller in its own right, what it does with the billing details you give
it. We receive reports and payouts rather than your card details, and for tax
purposes we invoice Polar rather than you. Its own privacy notice governs that
data.

**There is no CAPTCHA provider.** Sign-up is protected by a proof-of-work
check that your device computes and our own server verifies, so no third
party sees anything for it.

**An AI provider is named here before a feature that uses one ships**, never
after, and we will not offer a feature from a provider that does not
contractually agree that what is sent is not used to train its models.

## Transfers outside the European Union

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the
transfer is made under Chapter V GDPR on the basis of the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another adequate safeguard set
out in our data-processing agreement with that provider.

---

## Changes to this list

**We will give thirty (30) days' notice before a new sub-processor starts
processing personal data on our behalf**, by updating this page and — for
accountholders — by e-mail. A business customer under a data-processing
agreement may object to a new sub-processor within that period on reasonable
data-protection grounds; if we cannot resolve the objection, that customer may
terminate the affected part of the service and receive a pro-rata refund of
the unused prepaid period.

Replacing a provider with another that does the same job under equivalent
safeguards is treated the same way. Removing one is not, and takes effect when
it happens.

Every version of this list that has ever been in force stays published at its
own permanent address. This one is at
<https://legal.ripplefx.app/v/subprocessors-1.0/>.

Questions, or to be told when this list changes:
